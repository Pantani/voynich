Important external sources include:

- Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library / Yale Digital Collections, for
  Manuscript MS 408 catalogue records and images.
- Wikimedia Commons pages listed in
  `voynich-codex-project/data/commons_image_sources.csv`.
- Voynich Information Browser image preview pages listed in
  `voynich-codex-project/data/image_sources.csv`.
- Zandbergen-Landini IVTFF/EVA transcriptions from Voynich.nu, especially
  `ZL3b-n.txt`.
- Reddy and Knight's public Voynich comparison data referenced by
  `voynich-codex-project/scripts/download_sources.py`.

Downloaded binary images under `voynich-codex-project/images/raw/` are local
working assets and are ignored by Git. Check each source page for current
credit, reuse, and licensing requirements before redistributing image files.

See `voynich-codex-project/docs/fontes_e_citacoes.md` for research references
and source links used by the project.
